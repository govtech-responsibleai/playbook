# Evals for RAI principles

Evaluating across the five Responsible AI principles ensures your system is safe, robust, fair, private, and transparent. Choose the evaluation dimension that matches your identified risks.

<PrinciplesGrid />

## Evaluation guides by principle

- **[Safety evals](./safety.mdx)** — does it avoid harmful, prohibited, or adversarial behaviour?
- **[Robustness evals](./robustness.md)** — does it stay consistent, grounded, and usable with real input?
- **[Fairness evals](./fairness.md)** — does it treat affected groups consistently?
- **[Privacy evals](./privacy.md)** — does it avoid leaking PII or sensitive data?
- **[Agentic evals](./agentic-evals.mdx)** — for systems that plan, use tools, or take multi-step actions
