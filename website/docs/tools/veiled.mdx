# VEILED-SH

:::info[What's new on this page]

New in this release: VEILED-SH for evaluating AI responses to indirect distress in Singapore contexts.

:::

AI systems may miss **indirect, culturally situated distress** expressed through coded or hidden language. **Knowing how to respond is also deeply subjective**, and practitioners may disagree about what a reply should prioritise.

VEILED-SH is a Singapore-context benchmark for evaluating AI responses to indirect expressions of distress, self-harm, and suicidal ideation. It preserves differences in practitioner perspectives through separate reference policies.

## Our taxonomy

Developed with practitioner input, our taxonomy considers the deployment context, persona, and prompt. Each dimension supports multiple labels.

## Dataset

The [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/govtech/veiled-sh) has **10,003 rows with labels from six profiles**. Three profiles represent peer-support practitioners and three represent clinicians. These labels are judge-generated. Each row includes a prompt, persona context, and one of four settings: counsellor chat, forum post, search query, or triage tool.

Run the prompts against your AI system and compare its responses with the profiles for the relevant role. Report agreement per profile to see which response expectations your system meets and where it falls short.

## Extend it for your use case

Use the prompts, personas, and contexts as seed data for **multi-turn scenarios**. A user simulator can build on each case to test how your system responds as a conversation develops.

You can also ask practitioners in your organisation to annotate a prompt sample and define appropriate responses for your setting. Use those annotations to calibrate your own evaluator profiles and check them against held-out human annotations. See [Evaluation methods](../evaluating-ai-systems/methods.mdx#human-evaluation) for guidance.

These profiles support proxy evaluations that measure agreement with selected reference policies. Clinical safety still requires separate validation.
