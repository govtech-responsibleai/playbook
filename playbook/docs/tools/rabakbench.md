# RabakBench

RabakBench is a multilingual AI safety benchmark tailored to Singapore's linguistic landscape, covering Singlish, Chinese, Malay, and Tamil.

Use RabakBench to evaluate multilingual safety behavior for LLMs, guardrails, or AI applications.
