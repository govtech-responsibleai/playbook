# MinorBench

MinorBench is a benchmark for child-specific safety risks in LLM applications.

Use MinorBench when evaluating systems that may be used by minors or deployed in educational contexts. It covers child-specific risk categories such as danger, sexual content, profanities, hateful content, self-harm, and substance use.
