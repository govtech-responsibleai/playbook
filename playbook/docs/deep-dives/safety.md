# Safety

!!! info "About this page"

    This page is new in the upcoming Responsible AI Playbook release. It is a short Safety primer that points readers at the canonical safety evals, taxonomy, and benchmark tools. All content is new.

Safety focuses on whether AI systems avoid harmful, prohibited, adversarial, or socially unacceptable behavior.

Start with [Safety evals](../evaluating-risks/safety.md) for practical testing guidance. For deeper material, see:

- [Risk taxonomy](../defining-risks/risk-categories.md#govtech-risk-taxonomy)
- [MinorBench](../tools/minorbench.md) and [RabakBench](../tools/rabakbench.md) — GovTech-developed safety benchmarks
- [Litmus](../tools/litmus.md) and the [Responsible AI Benchmark](../tools/responsible-ai-benchmark.md) for automated support
